About TravelPulse

TravelPulse explains how to stay online while travelling: local SIMs, eSIMs, roaming, and wifi. It is a sister site of NetPulse, which has free tools for speed tests, DNS, and IP lookups, and of VPNPulse, which explains VPNs in ordinary language.

What this site is

Guides for a person who is about to fly and does not want a surprise bill. Thailand is the first country written up in full. We do not sell SIMs or run an eSIM service. We do not score providers, print star ratings, or pick a winner.

Links to eSIM providers

The eSIM guide links to the homepages of Airalo, Holafly, Nomad, and Saily. Next to each link, the page says the provider may pay us if you sign up through it. The longer note is the affiliate disclosure.
