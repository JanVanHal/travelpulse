Affiliate disclosure

Some links on TravelPulse point at eSIM providers and are set aside for affiliate links.

Last updated: October 2026

What that means

On the eSIM guide, each "See Thailand plans" button goes to that provider's own homepage: Airalo, Holafly, Nomad, or Saily. The same buttons appear where a guide sends you to buy a plan. If you sign up through one of those links once it is a live affiliate link, the provider may pay TravelPulse.

That payment does not change the price you pay them. It also does not change the facts printed on this site. We do not rank providers, and we do not print prices, because both move.

Where you will see it

Each provider button has a line next to it saying the provider may pay us, with a link back to this page. The button goes to that company's public homepage. If a new provider link is added, the same line will sit next to it.
