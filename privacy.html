Privacy Policy

What happens to information when you read TravelPulse. The pages are static files. There is no account to create here.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Pages you request

We do not ask you to register, and the pages do not include a tracker of our own. The host that serves the files may keep ordinary server logs, such as the address of the page and the IP address of the request.

The contact form

If you use the form, your name, email address, and message are sent to Web3Forms, which delivers them by email to the people who run this site. We use that message to reply. If you email contact@travelpulse.site directly, we receive whatever you put in that message.

Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and other advertising companies use cookies to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites. A cookie is a small file the browser stores. Those companies can use the cookie to show you ads they think fit your recent browsing.

You can opt out of personalized ads in Google's ad settings. How Google uses advertising cookies is described in Google's advertising technologies policy.

Third-party ad vendors

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this website and others. Those vendors have their own policies. We do not control the cookies they set when an ad loads.

Links to eSIM providers

The eSIM guide links to provider websites. If you follow one, that company may set its own cookie so it can tell the visit came from TravelPulse, once the link is an affiliate link. Their privacy policy, on their site, describes what they collect after you arrive. See the affiliate disclosure.

YouTube uploader app (tao-tao-uploader)

tao-tao-uploader is a small private app run by the owner of TravelPulse. It uses YouTube API Services only to upload videos to, and post or manage comments on, the owner's own YouTube channels: TravelPulse, Pulse Connect and Top 9 Trends.

It does not collect, store or use data from any other YouTube user or visitor. The Google sign-in (OAuth) tokens it needs are stored locally on the owner's own machine. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

By using a YouTube feature you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the app can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

Affiliate links

Some links go to NordVPN, NordPass, Saily and SafetyWing as affiliate links, and the site may earn a commission if you buy through them. See the affiliate disclosure.

Asking about your information

To ask what we hold from a message you sent, or to ask us to delete it, write to contact@travelpulse.site. Ad cookies are handled by the ad companies. The Google settings link above is the control for those.
