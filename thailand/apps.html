Essential apps

LINE is how Thailand messages. Grab is how it moves. The rest is food, maps, money, and air quality.

LINE

Everyone uses it: shops, hotels, tour desks, and some government offices. Install it before you need to message a driver.

LINE MAN, GrabFood, Robinhood

Food delivery. GrabFood and LINE MAN are the names you will see most. foodpanda stopped serving Thailand in May 2025 and pointed its users to Robinhood (foodpanda notice).

Grab and Bolt

Cars and motorbike taxis. Save the airport pickup pin in the app; do not follow touts in the hall.

Grab: App Store Grab: Google Play Bolt: App Store Bolt: Google Play

Maps and Translate

Google Maps offline areas for islands and parks. Translate camera mode for menus and signs.

Thai banking apps

Every major Thai bank has its own mobile banking app for QR (PromptPay) payments, transfers and bills, once you have an account (bank comparison). Visitors without an account can use TAGTHAi Easy Pay for PromptPay QR (details).

Give each banking app its own password

Use a different long password for each bank, and for Grab and LINE. A password manager makes them, fills them in, and syncs the vault to your phone and laptop. It will not fill a saved password on a page that only looks like the real site. On public wifi you are not typing those passwords in a cafe. If you lose the phone, the logins are still on your other device, so you can change the ones that matter.

Wise

Multi-currency card while you have no Thai account. Wise

12Go

Intercity buses, trains, ferries. 12Go

Agoda

Hotels and guesthouses, widely used for Thailand stays. Agoda

Air quality

IQAir or Air4Thai. Important in the North during the burning season (around February to April).

Tourist Police

Hotline 1155 (24/7, English) and the official "Thailand Tourist Police" app from the Royal Thai Police (Tourist Police, Thailand.go.th).

Numbers to save

  • Police 191 (also the general emergency line)
  • Fire 199
  • Ambulance 1669
  • Tourist Police 1155

Source: GOV.UK, Thailand: getting help, Tourist Police.
