What changed recently

From 15 September 2026 the 60-day visa exemption was revoked. Nationals of 60 countries now get 30 days for tourism. Visa on Arrival is limited to Azerbaijan, Belarus and Serbia. Land and sea visa-exempt entries are capped at two per calendar year, with exceptions for Malaysia, Brunei, Indonesia and Singapore.
